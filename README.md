Emberline: a round-based tactical FPS in a dusk harbor yard. You and two Warden bots hold Sites A and B against four Emberjack attackers carrying a charge — win rounds by elimination, by running the clock out, or by defusing the charge before it blows. Money earned each round buys rifles, armor and grenades in the buy menu; gunfire rewards stopping, crouching and aiming over spraying on the move. Single-player against bots, three.js, no build step, no network.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Click to play: WASD to move, mouse to look, click to fire, R to reload, B for the buy menu, E to defuse, 1/2 for weapons, 3/4/5 for frag, flash and smoke charges.
